# Star Citizen Economy Tracker (SCET)

A desktop tool for Windows to record and analyse your economy in Star Citizen: what you mine, refine, salvage, haul, sell and spend, all in one place.

> _Screenshot: Overview_

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/SCET-ArisCorp/SCET-Releases/releases/latest)**: run `SCET-Setup-<version>.exe` and follow the wizard.

- Windows 10 or 11, no Python or other software needed
- Choose your own installation folder, desktop shortcut on request
- Your data stays on your PC (`%APPDATA%\SCET`) and is kept on updates and uninstalls
- SCET checks for updates at start and installs them on request

## Features

### Overview and statistics
- Dashboard with key figures: stock, stock value, income, expenses and balance
- Live cards for running refinery orders, the active transport and recent sales
- Statistics with charts for any period: income, expenses, profit, mining yield, losses and more

> _Screenshot: Statistics_

### Mining and refinery
- Mining tours with ship, location, unloading place and running timer
- FPS, ROC and ship mining, each with its own list of materials
- Refinery orders with countdown ring, yield and notification when an order is ready

> _Screenshot: Mining tour_

### Salvage
- Salvage tours with wrecks, places, salvage crates, loot, items, components and costs

### Transport
- Transports with route animation, losses and results
- 3D load planning: every cargo grid of your ship including modules, crates placed automatically

> _Screenshot: Load plan_

### Warehouse
- Crates, loose amounts, units, items and components per location
- Stock journal and history for every change

> _Screenshot: Warehouse_

### Sales, missions and expenses
- Sales with prices, profit and cost tracking from mining to sale
- Live missions with rewards, blueprints, cargo and costs
- Expenses from every activity in one list

### Crew actions
- Group activities with crew members and ships
- Share calculator for splitting the profit

### Fleet and master data
- Your hangar with ship pictures, components, modules and cargo grids
- Master data for materials, ships, components and locations, editable and exportable

> _Screenshot: Hangar_

### And more
- German and English
- Light, dark and dark blue design with five accent colors
- Automatic daily backups, manual backups and restore
- Full change history

## Feedback

SCET is in beta. Bugs can happen and the master data may be incomplete. Please make regular backups (Settings → Backup).
